Build Tracker

Everything we build for a pickup, on one screen. The floor taps what it finishes, the TVs show whether we are on pace, and the office can answer "where is that order?" without walking the plant.

The three rules

1 · Tap it when it happens, not later

The board is only worth reading if it is true. A tap takes two seconds.

2 · Build in the order on the screen

#1 before #2. If that order is wrong, say so — do not quietly build something else.

3 · Stuck? Report it before you move on

A blocked order in red gets help. A skipped order nobody knows about does not.

Working an order

  1. Take the order at the top Under UP NEXT. It is #1 for a reason — the numbers come from the pickup sheet and the coordinator.
  2. Tap START when you begin The card moves to In progress. The office and the TVs see it straight away.
  3. Tap DONE when it is finished and checked The card turns green and moves to Done. Only tap Done when it is actually ready for the next department.
  4. Wrong tap? A message appears at the bottom with Undo for a few seconds. After that, use the ⋮ menu on the card to set any stage.

When you cannot work on it

  1. Tap the ⚠ button on that card
  2. Pick the reason Material shortage · Machine down · Rework / wrong cut · Waiting on another department · Missing info / paperwork · Short-staffed · Order change · Other.
  3. Add a note if it helps "Missing 2 screens" beats "Other". Keep it short.
  4. Move to the next order It is on the red list now and on both TVs. When it is sorted, tap Clear block.

Do not leave an order half-done with nothing said. If it is stuck, block it. That is the only way anybody finds out in time to fix it.

Counts every 2 hours

Orders finish in lumps. A count every couple of hours is what tells us, at 11 in the morning, whether the week is going to work.

When

9:30  ·  11:30  ·  1:30  ·  4:00

The buttons at the top of the tablet turn amber and pulse when a count is due. On the TV that block flashes red with a ! until it is entered.

How

  1. Tap the button for your station Along the top of the tablet, one per station that gets counted.
  2. Enter the running total for today Not since the last check-in — the whole day so far. If you have cut 22 vents today, enter 22.
  3. Save The TV updates within seconds.

V4T — who counts what

Two lines run side by side, and both have to finish. Only the stations in bold get counted; the rest still count toward the target.

LineStationsStorage
VentsCut → Built → Glazed~4 orders can wait
FramesCut to size → Punched → Built4 carts / 4 stations
AssemblyVents into frame, squared and checked → Screenedgoes to Mods

One tablet is enough. The lead walks the line at each check-in and enters all of them. If you would rather split it, any phone or spare tablet can sign in with the same V4T login and sit at the other end of the line — both feed the same board.

Mods — what a "mod" is

A mod is one modular wall. A wall is built from 2, 3 or 4 frames. A room is 2, 3 or 4 walls, and an order can have more than one room.

StationCountWhich is
Mod framesframesFrames built from CNC-cut stock and stacked. Fast — count every frame you finish.
Final mod (wall)walls (mods)Frames staged on the table, V4T in, caulked, mod panels in, screwed, vinyl fix added. One finished wall = one mod.

Count frames at the first station and walls at the second. Do not try to convert in your head — the app knows roughly 3 frames make a wall and works the rest out.

If you miss one

Enter it late — a late count is far better than none. The block goes amber instead of green, and the day's total is still right.

Nobody touches the TV. It is read-only and it looks after itself. Here is what it is telling you.

AMBERClose — within 15%

!The count is late. Someone needs to enter it.

Right side

  • ✓ FINISHED TODAY — every order finished today, in green, with the time. Clears overnight.

Move the mouse — a small bar appears with Reload. If the clock in the corner has stopped or it says Offline, the PC has lost wifi. Tell Riz.

Adding orders

Orders come in one at a time as they are sold. Get them in early — the plant can only plan what it can see.

  1. Sign in as logistics
  2. Tap "Add an order"
  3. Fill in what you know Tag name and dealer are required. Pickup date if it is set. Leave the rest for the office.
  4. Tick the departments that have work on it Only tick the ones that actually build something. Ticking Panel when there is no panel puts a job on their tablet that will never get done.
  5. Save It appears on those tablets immediately, at the end of the build order, and the coordinator numbers it.

Changing an order

  • Pickup date moved — change it on the order. Every tablet updates and re-sorts itself.
  • A department is no longer involved — untick it. Their job disappears; the rest carries on.
  • Order cancelled or pulled — tell the coordinator; cancelling is an admin job so it is logged.

Tag names

Use the tag exactly as it appears on the paperwork — BOUSQUET_169483 — so it matches the pickup sheet, the files and what the floor sees on the box.

Do not create the same order twice

Search the tag first. Two copies mean two sets of jobs and a count that is wrong all week.

Office

Two jobs: say when the paperwork is ready, and fill in the details that let the app work out how long an order takes.

Paperwork ready

  1. Open the order
  2. Tick "Paperwork ready" when the drawings and cut sheets are done
  3. The floor sees it Until it is ticked, the floor can see the order is coming but knows the paperwork is not there yet. It saves them chasing you.

Order details

From the order confirmation. These set how hard the room is, which feeds the estimates:

FieldChoices
ModsHow many mods / walls — the single most useful number you can enter.
Room shapeSquare · Pitched / soffit · Cathedral / gable
WindowsV4T · Vinyl fix · Vinyl trap / glass
Panels2" insulated · Double foam, protected · Full filler panel

Why it matters: a 14-mod cathedral with trap glass is not the same day's work as a 6-mod square. With these filled in, the coordinator's "Can we make it?" is real. Without them, it is a guess.

Files

Attach drawings and paperwork to the order. The floor opens them with 📎 Files — no more walking a print out to the line.

Quality

Reporting a defect

  1. Tap ⚑ Quality on the order Anyone on the floor can do this, not just Quality.
  2. Pick the department responsible Mods, V4T, Panel, Track or Door — whoever made it. Departments with no job on that order are greyed out.
  3. Pick the defect Wrong size / does not fit · Out of square · Glazing problem · Scratched / damaged · Wrong colour · Wrong machined part · Material shortage · Missing parts · Other.
  4. Send it back, or just record it Send back reopens that department's job and puts an amber ↩ Sent back tag on their card. Leave it off to record a defect you fixed on the spot — it still counts in the report.

The quality screen

  • Everything open, newest first, with order, department and defect.
  • Resolve when it is fixed and checked — that closes it and time-stamps it.
  • Nothing is deleted. Every report stays in the weekly report.

What it is for

Not blame — pattern. Five "out of square" from the same station in a month is a machine or a method problem, and that only shows up if the small ones get logged too.

Send back vs block

Send back = it was built wrong; the department who built it must fix it.
Block (⚠) = we cannot build it — missing material, machine down, waiting on someone.

Running the week

Monday — set the week up

  1. Import the pickup Grid → Import. Paste from Excel, or drop the .xlsx. Pick the pickup date you want — only the orders under it come in.
  2. Check the numbering #1, #2, #3 and so on, in sheet order. Move an order up or down and every tablet follows. A moved order is badged on the floor so nobody misses it.
  3. Set the crew Overview → Crew today → people per station. Without this there is no target and the TVs stay blank.
  4. Read "Can we make it?" Person-days needed against person-days available. Act on Monday, not Thursday.

Every morning — 2 minutes

  • Needs attention — blocked jobs and open quality issues.
  • Crew today — who is actually in. It changes the targets.
  • Ready for pickup — are we where we should be for the day?

Targets and stations

Enter minutes, not rates

For each station, enter how many minutes it takes one person to make one. That is what a stopwatch measures. The app does the rest.

Daily target = people × (7.5 working hours × 60 ÷ minutes for one)

7.5 hours is 7:30 to 4:00 less the three breaks. The app never counts break time or weekends against anyone.

The columns

ColumnWhat to put
StationThe step's name, in the order work flows.
UnitWhat is counted there — vents, inserts, mods.
Minutes for oneOne person, one unit. It shows you the per-hour rate underneath.
Per finishedHow many of that unit make one finished thing. 4 vents per V4T insert. 1 for everything else — a 2 here halves your output.
BufferHow many orders can wait after that station — the 4 carts, the 4 stations. Reference only.
CountTicked = the tablet asks for a count there. Untick the ones you do not need a number from; they still count toward the target.

Parallel lines

V4T's vents and frames run side by side and both have to finish, so the slowest station anywhere sets the department's output. The app names it as the bottleneck — that is where the next person goes.

The weekly report

Report tab → download. Eight sheets: summary, orders, department jobs, blocks, quality, people, crew and the full activity log. Durations are in working hours, so an overnight block does not read as 16 hours.

Admin only

The report and the names of who built what are on the admin login alone. Do not hand that login out.
